    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, but nothing except one meditation spot can be missed. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save: they switch off trophies and saving.

    ⚠ The one possible missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 is gone after that chapter. Do it, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.
